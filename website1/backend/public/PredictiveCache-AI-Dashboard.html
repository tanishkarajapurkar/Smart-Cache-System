PredictiveCache AI Executive Pitch & Technical Thesis

Why Classical LRU Fails in Cloud Architectures & How We Solve It

1. The Problem with Classical LRU / LFU

Classical caching algorithms treat all keys as computationally equal. In modern cloud architectures (Node.js + Supabase/Postgres), a cache miss incurs real financial egress and compute costs ($0.00015 / DB read + CPU parsing).

Worse, classical LRU is highly vulnerable to Cache Pollution: a periodic web crawler, inventory scan, or reporting script accesses thousands of cold records once, kicking hot commercial products out of RAM. This results in devastating latency spikes and sudden cloud billing surges.

2. The Solution: Cost-Aware Predictive Utility

PredictiveCache AI introduces an intelligent machine-learning cost-awareness function. Rather than blindly caching based on timestamp recency, it calculates the net financial utility U(k) of every resource:

U(k) = P(re-access) × FetchCost - MemoryCost × Size - LatencyPenalty

If a query is predicted to have low probability of subsequent access, it is serviced directly without evicting high-value commercial items. If an item has surging velocity, its TTL is dynamically extended and proactively refreshed.
